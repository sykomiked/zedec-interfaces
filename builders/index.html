You may charge a fee

Yes. Take a fair, minimal fee out of the transaction flow.

Two clean ways: wrap the action in your own contract and show the fee on the confirmation screen, or charge for the interface itself rather than the transaction.

What we ask: state the fee before the user signs, keep it minimal, and never take it out of collateral or out of a redemption someone is depending on.

We will not take a cut, we cannot revoke you, and there is no gate to pass — the core has no owner to ask.
