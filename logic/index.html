The four verdicts

TRUEverified, consistent — settles
FALSErefuted — rejected
BOTHtwo valid claims that conflict — held
NEITHERnothing verifiable — held

Why two values fail

When two valid, conflicting claims arrive, a conventional system resolves by rule: last writer wins, majority wins, or timelock then default. All three destroy information. After resolution nobody can tell the system was ever uncertain.

If two attestors disagree and both are cryptographically valid, one is lying or one is wrong. Settling on either releases value on a claim the system cannot verify. Holding costs time; settling wrongly costs money.

The eight problems it answers

This single design decision is the answer to eight different problems:

  • disputed escrow — both parties claim success
  • coverage — half-settled transfer
  • solver reputation — two credible attestations disagree
  • futarchy — market cannot resolve cleanly
  • agent spending limits — policy rules conflict
  • activity feed — relays disagree about an event
  • double-booking — two valid reservations
  • disputed data payment — settled payment, open dispute
