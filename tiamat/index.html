The mechanism

There was no premine, no ICO, no private sale, no team allocation, no treasury, no emissions. Every PQOS in existence was created by someone depositing NEAR.

backing per new TIAMAT = phi^(21 × supply / cap) NEAR

Because the exponent rises with supply, collateral per token rises monotonically. Issuance is bounded by collateral, not by time. Redemption burns the token and returns a pro-rata slice of the whole reserve.

The thing everyone gets wrong

Minting credits an internal GRIDCHAIN account, not a wallet.

deposit → internal balance
export → NEP-141 wallet

A user who mints and sees nothing has not lost anything — the tokens are in the ledger, one export away.
